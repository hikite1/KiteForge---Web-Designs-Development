Common questions

Do I own my website?

Yes. The domain and all your content are yours. If you ever move on, we hand everything over. No lock‑in.

How long does a build take?

A typical small site goes live a few weeks after we have your branding and content. Timelines depend on how fast revisions and material come back.

What do you need from me to start?

Your logo and brand colors, the text and photos for each section, and any menus or PDFs.

Can I make changes after launch?

Managed plans include one or two small edits a month. Full Service covers unlimited edits within your existing pages. New sections or features are quoted separately.

How do I pay?

You get the full invoice when you approve the build, but nothing is due until the site is delivered. Monthly hosting and upkeep is billed each month, through Square.
